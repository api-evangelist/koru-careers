**Status: defunct.** Koru (joinkoru.com) was a Seattle-based predictive hiring and people-analytics SaaS company. Its platform built a per-employer "predictive hiring fingerprint" and scored candidates against the Koru7 Impact Skills assessment (Grit, Rigour, Impact, Teamwork, Curiosity, Ownership, Polish) through a 20-minute online pre-interview that produced a Candidate Fit Score. Products were marketed as Koru Hire and Koru Develop.

Koru was acquired by **Cappfinity**. Between February and November 2020 joinkoru.com began redirecting to `koru.cappfinity.com`, where the Koru7 Impact Skills Assessment was carried as a Cappfinity product. That subdomain now redirects to `cappfinity.com`, which carries no remaining Koru branding.

> **Warning:** the **joinkoru.com** domain has lapsed and currently serves unrelated online-gambling affiliate content. It no longer belongs to this company — do not link to it.

Koru published no public developer portal, documentation, or API, so this repo carries no technical artifacts. The full enrichment evidence chain (live probes, DNS, and archived snapshots) is recorded in [`review.yml`](review.yml).
